We moved to Santa Cruz back in 1997 and I always thought we would live here
forever. We raised our three children, made the best friends, and rode my
bicycle on every road this great place has to offer. Charlotte and I are
retired, Gabe is living in Golden, Colorado, and the pull of our old love of
Colorado is drawing us back.

Our yard has always been the most special part of our home. We always called it
Multer State Park, and many a school field trip made this a place of
adventure.
